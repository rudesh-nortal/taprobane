Frequently Asked Questions

How should I brew Taprobane tea?

Use freshly boiled water (let it cool for 1 minute). Steep for 5-7 minutes. For best results, drink without milk or sugar to experience the full herbal flavors.

When will I see results?

Many customers notice improvements within 3-5 days of regular use. For best results, make it part of your daily routine.

Are there any side effects?

Our teas are made from natural herbs and are generally safe for most people. If you're pregnant, nursing, or taking medication, please consult your doctor first.

How long does tea stay fresh?

When stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, our teas remain fresh for 12-18 months.
